Before you submit

FAQ

Here's everything you need to know before you put your company name on the list.

Do I have to be a Housecall Pro customer to enter?

No. This is open to any U.S.-based home service business owner.

What do I need to submit?

Your company name and a brand visual—a logo, truck or van wrap, shop, sign, or a photo of your crew. No logo yet? You'll still be able to enter with a placeholder graphic.

Is there a cost to enter?

No. No entry fee, no purchase necessary.

Can I write about how I came up with the name?

Yes. It won't affect your entry.

How does voting work?

Voting opens August 17 and runs through September 7. Anyone can vote once for their favorite names. The Top 50 is determined by total votes.

What happens after I submit?

We review every entry for basic eligibility before voting opens. Compliant entries move on automatically.

Is this a sweepstakes?

Yes—the grand prize is awarded by vote (a contest), and there's a separate $1,000 prize awarded to one random voter (a sweepstakes). No purchase necessary to enter or win either one. Full details in the Official Rules.
